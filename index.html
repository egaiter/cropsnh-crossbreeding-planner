CropsNH Breeding Path Finder

Static single-page app over cropsnh_breeding.db — queried with SQLite compiled to WebAssembly. No backend, no network.

Paths chain one crop into the next; extra parents per step must be sourced separately.
🌱 item marks crops plantable directly from that item with no breeding; tied-shortest paths are prioritized so fewer ingredients still need breeding.
